Town Halls

Where you fit in the plan

These cards summarise how I think about each key Town Hall band. As guides launch, they’ll link to detailed breakdowns for each level.

TH 10–11
Foundation
  • Unlock core heroes and basic spell combinations.
  • Learn simple funneling and spell timing.
  • Build habits that won’t break at higher THs.
TH 12–13
Transition
  • Deal with Giga Tesla / Giga Inferno properly.
  • Balance heroes, lab and offense upgrades.
  • Start thinking about war and CWL roles.
TH 14–15
Endgame
  • Max heroes and pets without killing your storage.
  • Use a small set of strong, reliable armies.
  • Switch between farming, pushing and wars smartly.
