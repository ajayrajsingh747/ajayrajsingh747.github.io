Town Halls

Where your account sits in the journey

I think in bands, not just one level. These bands guide how aggressive your upgrade and attack plans should be.

TH 10–11 Foundation
  • Learn basic funneling and spell timing.
  • Build core hero habits (no hero suicide).
  • Use this stage to fix bad upgrade habits.
TH 12–13 Transition
  • Respect Giga Tesla / Giga Inferno properly.
  • Balance heroes, lab and big defence jumps.
  • Start contributing in CWL, not just casual wars.
TH 14–15 Endgame
  • Heroes, pets and key defences become priority.
  • Swap between farming, pushing and war modes.
  • Small mistakes matter more — planning is critical.
